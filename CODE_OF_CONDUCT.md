# Code of Conduct

sealwax adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, as
its code of conduct. Its full text is at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.

## Our pledge, in short

We want taking part in sealwax to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their
age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We expect everyone to be respectful and considerate, to accept constructive criticism gracefully,
and to focus on what is best for the project and its users. Harassment, insults, personal or
political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers, at the contact address shown
on the [sealwax organization's profile](https://github.com/sealwax). Every report is reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the maintainers respect the privacy and security of the
reporter.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the code of conduct may face temporary or permanent
repercussions, as determined by other members of the project's leadership.
